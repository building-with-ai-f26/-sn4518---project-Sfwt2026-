# FlexWeek

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who lift on a weekly schedule and also play other sports, starting with the builder and MBA friends. They plan on desktop or phone and may log on a phone during training.

## Product Purpose

Help someone see their training week at a glance and know which workout to start. Keep the interface quiet during a session so it is easy to record sets, reps, and weight between exercises.

## Positioning

FlexWeek keeps a reusable base plan while letting lifters adapt a dated workout and see a short reason for each change.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The app supports profile and base-week setup, dated sessions, workout changes, exercise substitutions, logging, and saved history. The deployed app uses fixed rules and browser storage. The current workout UI assumes full-gym equipment.

## Brand Commitments

Use the existing FlexWeek name. Keep decoration from competing with workout information or controls.
